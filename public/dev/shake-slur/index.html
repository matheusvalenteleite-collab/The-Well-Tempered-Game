Ornament slurs: a trill as one sound

Four instruments, each on a real trill from a real piece, played by the game itself (the real engine, phone sound, your line alone). In each pair, A is the game today and B is the slurred ornament. The notes and their times are the print's, exactly the same in A and B; only how the instrument speaks them changes: one sound that moves from note to note, instead of a new attack on each.

In each picture, the top half is pitch (grey blocks = the printed notes of the trill, blue line = what you hear) and the bottom half is loudness (yellow; in B the dashed grey line is A, for comparison). Tap a picture to jump there. Nothing here is in the game: it is all behind a switch that is off.

What to decide

For each of the four: is B (the slur) what that instrument's trill should sound like? Yes, no, or change it.

A note on loudness: in A the trilled notes sit a few dB below the held note after the trill (each re-struck note starts from silence). In B the trill sits at the held note's level, as a slurred note would. The score never sets the loudness; this is only the instrument's own envelope.
