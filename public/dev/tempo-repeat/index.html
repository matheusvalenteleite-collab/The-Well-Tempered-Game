Tempo changes and the REPEAT button: a sketch

Developer-only throwaway. Not part of the game and not wired to it. The tune is an invented stand-in, not Bach. It only shows how the clock, the music box, the timing window and the REPEAT button could behave. Needs sound. Companion to docs/research/TEMPO-AND-REPEAT-DESIGN.md.

The stand-in has an ADAGIO (bars 1 to 4), an ALLEGRO (bars 5 to 12, a section with a repeat), a coda (bars 13 to 16) and a slowing into the last bar. In bars 11 and 12 the REPEAT button appears, and goes one beat before the section ends. Tap the left and right bars (or press F and J) along with the notes; SPACE also presses REPEAT. The pale band at the play line is your timing window: it is always 200 ms, so it looks wider in the Adagio and narrower in the Allegro.
