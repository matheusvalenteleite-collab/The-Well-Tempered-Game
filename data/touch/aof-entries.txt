# THE ART OF FUGUE'S ENTRIES (D707): every statement of each piece's themes, the table the game's entries are
# built from (tools/touch/more-bach.ts for BWV 1080/1-17, tools/touch/extract.ts for Contrapunctus 14; both place
# it in the notes with tools/touch/expert.ts placeExpert, as the 48's data/wtc/expert-entries.txt, D493).
# No published analysis of the work is in the repo; the statements are read from the notes by
# tools/touch/aof-entries/scan.py (table.py drafted this table from its finds) and reviewed bar by bar:
#   S  scan.py: the theme's letter steps and rhythm laid from every note of every voice (prime and inverted; at
#      its values, doubled, halved); kept where its first six notes fit (one step off by one at most: a tonal
#      answer) and at least 0.8 of all its notes (fit printed); or the same from its second note (a first note
#      changed). "mod" entries are the earlier finders' (D340) that the scan's threshold misses (an interval bent);
#      "at" entries are variants placed where they start (the subject's thirds filled, in C3), read by eye.
#   G  the game's own entry (D189's finders for Contrapunctus 14), confirmed by scan.py unless noted.
# Grammar as data/wtc/expert-entries.txt, and (D707):
#   dux / comes: a canon's leader and follower (each restatement of the canon's head marked again);
#   at: the statement starts on the note at BAR+Q (no fitting), n= notes long;
#   "upside=T,T" in a piece's header: those themes' first statements (the table's shapes) are the inversion of the
#   cycle's own form (the main subject as Contrapunctus 1 states it; Contrapunctus 8's two subjects as there),
#   so every statement of them is told inverted or not against that form, not against the piece's first.
# Each theme is read from the line marked "read here" (its len=, quarter notes), whatever comes first in time.
# A scale is told against the theme's values in that line: Contrapunctus 6's and 7's from a statement in minims
# (the subject's values in 5-7), so 7's crotchet statements are its diminutions and its semibreves augmentations.
# Themes: 1, 2, 3 by first entry. C8: 1 the new subject (b. 1), 2 the repeated-note subject (39), 3 the main
# subject varied, inverted (94). C9: 1 the quick subject, 2 the main subject in long values (35). C10: 1 its own
# subject, 2 the main subject varied, inverted (23). C11: 1 the main subject varied, 2 C8's first subject
# inverted (27), 3 C8's second (97). Contrapunctus 14: 1, 2 (114), 3 B-A-C-H (193), as D189.
