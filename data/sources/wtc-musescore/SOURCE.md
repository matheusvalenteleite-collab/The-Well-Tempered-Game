# Source: verits99's Well-Tempered Clavier, Book II (MuseScore)

- The Well-Tempered Clavier, Part II, as uploaded to musescore.com by **verits99** (user 13172,
  https://musescore.com/user/13172), the set "The Well-Tempered Clavier Part II (A= 440Hz)" (2013),
  each prelude and fugue a score, as kept in the PDMX dataset (Long, Novack, Berg-Kirkpatrick and
  McAuley, *PDMX: A Large-Scale Public Domain MusicXML Dataset*, 2024; zenodo.org/records/14648209, CC BY 4.0), MusicXML (`.mxl`). Fetched 27 October
  2026. The uploader names no source edition. Made for playback: no ornament signs; trills written out;
  repeats written out.
- **Licence: CC0 1.0**, as PDMX records musescore.com's metadata for every one of these scores:
  `license: cc-zero`, "Creative Commons copyright waiver"
  (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), `license_conflict: False`. Known only through
  that scrape (the profile page was not reachable to check). Credited though CC0 asks for nothing.
- **Provenance** (`index.json` beside this file): for each file the MuseScore score id and page, the
  uploader, the licence string, the creation and update dates, the revision, the PDMX paths (`mxl`,
  `metadata`).
- **Used for** Book II's preludes 3-5 and 7-24 (`wtc2pNN-<id>.mxl`; nos. 1, 2 and 6 come from the
  Mutopia Project, public domain: data/wtc/sources/mutopia). Read by `tools/wtc/sources.ts`: the two
  staves as the two hands; the written-out repeats taken once, as printed (a repeated section found from
  the notes, three bars in four alike; its differing last bars the endings, the piece's last section
  ending once); the written-out trills (six or more demisemiquavers between two pitches a step apart)
  folded back into their note, with a trill sign (an appoggiatura or Nachschlag in them is lost); II/5's
  triplet quavers for the dotted quaver and semiquaver written as dotted, as the game always played
  them; II/13's missing time signature is 3/4; II/22 and II/24 keep 4/4 (verits99 writes 2/2).
- **Witnesses only** (not shipped; the cross-check of `tools/wtc/sources-check.ts`): `wtc2p01/02/06`,
  verits99's Book II fugues (`fugues/`), and verits99's scores at A = 415 Hz (`a415/`, the prelude and
  fugue in one, Books I and II, written at A = 440 pitch; Book I's an independent witness of OpenWTC's).
- **Not proofread against the Bach-Gesellschaft** (the scans were not reachable from here): where
  verits99's Book II preludes differ from the Humdrum cross-check (docs/wtc/SOURCES-CHECK.md lists
  every place) nothing decides between them; they are left as verits99 has them. To check against the
  BGA (IMSLP, Band 14) before Book II's preludes are relied on.
