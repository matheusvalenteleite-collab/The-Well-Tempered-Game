# The Algomus reference analysis of Bach's fugues

`fugues.ref`: the Algomus team's (CRIStAL, Lille / MIS, Amiens) reference analysis of the 24 fugues of WTC I (and 12
Shostakovich fugues, not used), fetched 12 October 2026 from https://gitlab.com/algomus.fr/algomus-data
(`fugues/fugues.ref`; changelog to 2016/04). Compiled from Prout (1910), Tovey (1924), Keller (1965), Bruhn (1993),
Charlier (2009) and the team's own analysis.
