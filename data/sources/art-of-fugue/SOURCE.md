# Source: Bach, Die Kunst der Fuge, the unfinished Fuga a 3 soggetti (BWV 1080)

- `contrapunctus-xiv/contrapunctusXIX.mid` and `.ly`: the Mutopia Project, piece 766
  (https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=766), fetched 22 October 2026.
  Typeset by Arnaud Gossart in LilyPond from the Breitkopf & Härtel edition of 1885; last updated
  2006. Mutopia numbers it "Contrapunctus XIX" (after the Bach-Gesellschaft's numbering); it is the
  unfinished fugue on three subjects usually numbered Contrapunctus XIV today. Four voices, D minor, 4/4.
- **Rights.** Public domain: the typesetter placed it in the public domain (Creative Commons "No rights
  reserved"), as the file's header and Mutopia's page state. Unlike the WTC encoding (bach-wtc), it may
  be published freely.
