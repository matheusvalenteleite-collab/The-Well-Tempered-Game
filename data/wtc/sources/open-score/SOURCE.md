# Source: an open-score edition of the 48 fugues

Kyle Rother, *An 'open source' open score edition of Bach's Well-tempered Clavier* (University of Cape
Town, OpenUCT, 2015), https://open.uct.ac.za/items/d2a6887b-7196-4fbe-8b56-a469bf557f93.

- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The files' own headers say "This edition is in the public
  domain, and the editor does not claim any rights in the content" and "No rights reserved".
- **Text:** typeset in LilyPond from the 1866 Breitkopf & Härtel Bach-Gesellschaft Ausgabe (Kroll), with
  reference to the Henle and Bärenreiter Urtext editions and Dürr's commentary (the editor's note in each
  file).
- **What is here:** the LilyPond source of each of the 48 fugues (`book1/fugueNN.ly`, `book2/fugueNN.ly`;
  the editor's `fugueN_expanded.ly`, or `fugue10_basic.ly` for Book I no. 10, renamed) and the one prelude of
  the edition (Book I no. 7, `book1/prelude07.ly`). Open score: each voice on a staff of its own.
- **Downloaded:** 2026-10-10, `bach_WTCbook1_openscore.zip` and `bach_WTCbook2_openscore.zip` from the
  repository item above. The PDFs and the cover notes are not copied.
