Generated by `node tools/lab/habits-study.ts`. At every choice of Fux's two-voice solutions
(species 1-4) where the rules leave more than one pitch, a model of his habits ranks the legal
pitches; it is learnt from his other solutions only (the exercise judged is left out). Two
measures: the mean surprise at the note Fux wrote, in bits (lower is better; with no model at all
it is log2 of the number of legal pitches), and how often his note is ranked first, alone.
