# The literature, shared by all sessions (D161)

One list, so that the sessions' reviews do not duplicate each other. Every entry is checked before it is
listed (author, title, venue, year, pages where they matter); nothing is listed from memory alone. Each
session adds its own rows and leaves the others' alone. The owner chooses what is read.

**Tier.**
- **1:** read now; it answers a question the session is working on.
- **2:** consult for a named question.
- **3:** known and judged not relevant, with the reason, so that it is not proposed again.

**Status.** One of: proposed / chosen by the owner / read / not available.

**Access.** One of: open (with the place) / library / not found.

## INVENTIONS: the Two-Part Inventions and Three-Part Sinfonias, BWV 772-801 (I0, 12 October)

Searched 12 October 2026 in four parts: sources and history; *inventio* and the period's theory; analysis
and pedagogy; computational and corpus work. The narrative and the proposal are in
`docs/inventions/LITERATURE-REVIEW.md`.

**How the rows were checked.** Every row was matched by a search against one of these: a publisher page, a
Crossref DOI record, a library catalogue (DNB, RISM, university catalogues), BachBib, or the text itself.
Where a part is unconfirmed, the row says which part ("pp. unconfirmed", "content not seen"). The
**question** column states what the item is *known* to address. Where that rests on an abstract, a review
or a citing author rather than the text, the column says so.

**Access.** "open" gives the place. "library" means print or subscription; most items marked library are
on JSTOR, Oxford Academic or De Gruyter.
