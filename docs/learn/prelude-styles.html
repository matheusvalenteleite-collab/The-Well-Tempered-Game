Ways to play a prelude

Four of the play styles proposed in preludes.md, each shown on the real opening bars of Prelude 1 in C major (BWV 846), notes taken from the game's own data (data/touch/pieces.json, wtc1p01), at the game's tempo for it (♩ = 55). The screen is the phone held sideways: the music box in the middle, notes flowing left to the gold line; the two thumb strips at the edges, higher notes higher. Press ▶ to hear it and to see what the player would touch.

left thumb right thumb a held "sung" line the game plays silent until caught

1 · The reveal (bars 1–11)

The newcomer's first minute. Bars 1–4: one touch a bar, the left thumb on the bass at the downbeat, held for two beats. Bars 5–8: the right thumb joins on the top note of the figure (beat 2). Bars 9–11, to the cadence in G: bass on beats 1 and 3, top on beats 2 and 4, one touch a beat, the thumbs taking turns. Everything else the game plays, and everything is always visible.

2 · Outer voices (bars 1–4)

The two thumbs as the two edges of the music: the left thumb holds the bass (a tail as long as the note), the right thumb taps the top of each figure. This is the version the owner already found "much better" (D261); here it is the backbone the other styles build on.

3 · Catch the layers (bars 1–6)

The box starts nearly empty: only the figure's middle notes sound. Play a layer cleanly for one bar and it is caught: it goes on by itself while you catch the next. Bar 1 the bass, bar 2 the top, bar 3 the tenor; bar 4 you rest and listen to all you built; in bar 5 the bass starts to fade and must be caught again. Dashed notes are silent, waiting.

4 · Slow strands (bars 1–8)

Each bar of this prelude is one chord of five notes, and each of the five, followed from bar to bar, is a slow tune of one note a bar. Pick a strand: the player holds its note for the bar (heard as a sung tone over the figure), keeps holding where it stays, and lifts and moves where it moves. Bars 1–4 every moving strand moves by a step; bars 5–8 the upper strands leap as the chord is spread wider.

Simple synthesized tones, not the game's sound. The player's notes are a little brighter and louder, as in the game (D252). Timing, pitches and bars are the data's; the choice of what the player touches is the proposal.
