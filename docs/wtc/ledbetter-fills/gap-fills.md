# Ledbetter gap fills (from pdftotext full.txt)

Keyed to the four gap lists: same order, same ids, printed pages. FILLED = located and paraphrased. DISPLACED = the words are in the conversion but somewhere else. NOT FOUND = not recoverable (usually a music-font glyph also lost in the PDF text). Accidentals in [] are inferred, not printed. Time-signature glyphs: in the raw text ^D = C; cut-C (alla breve) is usually blank. "Claims:" lines are suggested additions in the digest grammar.

## wtc1p01 / wtc1f01

## wtc1p02 / wtc1f02
- **wtc1p02** p.151: FILLED. Some have read "the first five of the Clavier-Büchlein Praeludia as variations on a common harmonic basis" (Niedt; n.29 Poulin and Taylor). L.: true only in a general way. The bass descends to a dominant pedal with a single station, E♭ at b.14.

- **wtc1f02** p.154 (invention principle): FILLED. All the material "grows from the subject": one of the most densely developmental fugues. The interlude bb.22–5 does not modulate (reprise feel). bb.25–6 are in effect a dominant pedal.
- **wtc1f02** n.33: FILLED. The earlier piece is J. Krieger's Toccata ex D (Ex.7.6). Fischer's B♭ Fuga is an interim stage.
- **wtc1f02** n.36: FILLED. The note is attached at b.28½, so the added alto f', e' are in b.28.
