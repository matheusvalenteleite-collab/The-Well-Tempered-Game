# The original claims, checked against the encoded score

The 401 claims taken from the Markdown conversion were first compared only with the game's
analysis. After the merge of the fills, every claim the analysis did not confirm, and every prelude
claim (the analysis covers fugues only), was read in the notes: 199 claims. The other 292 either
held in the analysis (229) or had been checked above (63).

- **168 confirmed** (where a claim had `voice?`, the voice is now named).
- **22 corrected**, marked `(score; …; L.: …)`. Most are placement: Ledbetter gives the bars where
  an entry mostly lies rather than where its upbeat begins (Book I no. 3, bb.3 and 5 for his "bb.4–5"
  and "bb.6–7"), or the bar of the dominant rather than the arrival of a cadence (Book II preludes 2,
  17, 22). Some are errors in the digest's conversion of his prose into claims (Book II no. 21: his
  pedals are in the middle part and the cantus, not the bass). One is an error in his text: Book II
  no. 24 prelude, the "subdominant reprise" at b.41 is in F sharp minor, the dominant.
- **9 undecidable**, flagged `[check]`: they concern early versions (BWV 870a, 872a, 875a; added
  passages in Book I preludes 4, 7, 12), which the encoding does not contain. In each case the
  final version is consistent with his account of the revision.
- None absent from the score.

The analysis's misses among these were almost all its own: sections, cadences, strettos, entries
with varied heads, second subjects and inverted entries that are plainly in the notes.
