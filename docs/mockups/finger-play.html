FINGER PLAY · A RUN OF FOUR NOTES ON THE LADDER

Above: the phone held sideways, the two side bars; each circle is a thumb on the glass. Below: a timeline of what sounds. The music never stops, so the next note must land before the last is let go.

✕ ONE THUMB

The thumb must lift, travel and land: a gap between every note. Detached; no legato bonus; hard to keep up in fast runs.

✓ TWO THUMBS, ALTERNATING

Each thumb lands while the other still holds, then the other lets go. The notes join: LEGATO ×2, ×3… scores a growing bonus. The idle thumb has a whole note's time to get to its next place.

left thumb right thumb overlap: legato (a brief moment is enough) silence between notes
