| D300 | The owner on D298's sheet mode: (1) "it's not good that the music disappears after played … The visible sheet scrolling should make both things to come and things played visible, not only things to come"; (2) "the subject can be further highlighted by a background of some shade"; (3) "In the one staff per voice mode, we don't need color coding as much as with the redux mode … a more sober less colourful interface"; (4) "the kern engravings strike me as a bit childish and too round.
