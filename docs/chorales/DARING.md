# Daring: Bach's licences in the generator (X5, X6)

Generated by `node tools/chorales/daring_report.ts`. The daring control (`src/chorale/generate/daring.ts`) raises a choice by
2^(λ × share), the share being how often Bach's own use of that choice in that context is a licence of the catalogue
(docs/chorales/LICENCES.md), λ the weight of the licence's group: a chord after the chord before over the melody's degree, a
bass and its position under the chord (the diminished seventh chord is now a position the generator can choose), a bass
move, a cadence chord for the melody's close. Rarity alone is never raised. The tilt is applied to whole settings (the joint
chain draws each setting in proportion to its probability times the tilt of all its choices), and the draws are ranked by
both.

## Calibration

The weights are set so that **level 1 writes each group of licences at Bach's own rate** in the 370 chorales, **level 2 at
twice it, level 3 at three times**; level 0 is the plain generator. They are found by iteration (each weight moved by the
log ratio of the target rate to the rate written) on the melodies of a fifth of the tunes (68 chorales, each set twice),
with models and a lexicon that have seen neither those melodies nor the test ones.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| chromatic-bass (Chromatic bass lines) | 0.42 | 4.19 | 5.7 | 6.66 |
| mixture (Mixture: the minor mode's chords in a major region; The Neapolitan: the flat second in its local key) | 0.31 | -2.62 | -1.24 | -0.33 |
| diminished-seventh (Diminished sevenths of another degree) | 0.56 | 0.45 | 0.59 | 0.69 |

| distant-close (Closes in distant keys) | 0.19 | 1.11 | 4.8 | 8.5 |
| open-close (Phrases that end on a dissonant chord) | 0.08 | 5.2 | 14 | 14 |
