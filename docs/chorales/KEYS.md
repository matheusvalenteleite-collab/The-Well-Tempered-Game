## The reading

Two levels, as an analyst reads them:

- **Regions**: a hidden Markov chain over the 24 keys, one state per on-beat chord, decoded by Viterbi. A region's key
  must be confirmed by a close: a phrase may be in the home key, or in a key whose tonic or dominant ends it or ends the
  phrase before (a modulation starts in one phrase and closes in the next). Within those, a key is told by the pitches
  sounding (Temperley's key profiles), by whether the chord belongs to it (the melodic minor's chords too), by a dominant
  going to its tonic, and by a phrase end on its tonic or dominant; against a cost for changing key and for keys far
  from home by the circle of fifths. The chain starts at home and ends at home (a chorale ending on the dominant ends
  with a half or a Phrygian close, not in the dominant's key).
- **Tonicisations** inside a region: a chord outside the region's key that is a dominant (major, dominant seventh, or
  the diminished seventh read as a dominant with a minor ninth) or a leading-tone chord (diminished) of a degree of the
  key and goes to it is *applied* to that degree (V/ii, vii°/V). A minor key's tonic as a major chord at a phrase end
  (the Picardy third) is in the key. What is left is *chromatic* in its local key: the licences' ground.

Weights (set by hand): pitch 1, inKey 2, outKey -4, dominant 4, tonicEnd 6, fullClose 12, dominantEnd 2, change 9, distance 0.6, last 30.
