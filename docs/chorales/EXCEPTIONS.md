# Bach's exceptions, X1: a map of surprise

Generated by `node tools/chorales/surprise_report.ts`. Every choice in Bach's 370 chorales is measured by the generator's
models trained without its tune (`src/chorale/generate/`): its surprise in bits (-log2 of the probability the model gave it;
1 bit is a coin toss, 7 bits about 1 in 128) and what the model expected. Rarity is not yet beauty: the most surprising
moments are candidates, to be heard and rated (the rating page), then sorted into the avoided, the marked, the merely rare
opportunity and the slip (docs/chorales/PLAN.md, X2-X4).

Time base checked: Bach's on-beat events and the study's soprano agree at 100% of 16511 onsets.

only where the sonority is dissonant (a second, fourth, seventh or ninth over the bass, a tritone, an augmented sixth,

given the melody degree and the place in the phrase. The inner voices
