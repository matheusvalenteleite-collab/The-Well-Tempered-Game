# The 48 fugues' names, pictures and looks (the owner's choices, 31 October 2026)

Chosen by the owner over two rounds of the studio's proposals (claude.ai pages "The 48 names" and "round two";
stores `choices` and `choices2`). Supersedes D374/D383's names where they differ. Each line: name · picture ·
the look on the play screen (strips' colours · one shape · one quiet moment from the music's data). Looks are
proposals still to be built and checked (the system: the studio page "Each fugue's own look").

## Standing rule for every picture (the owner, 31 Oct)

"Don't make all pictures so manly, strive for gender variety, don't use masculine as neutral or default."
Across the 48, women, men and androgynous figures in balance; no figure male by default; groups mixed.

## Book I

1. **The Canon** · four singers (women and men) in a staggered row, each mouth opening a beat after the last · stone and candle-gold strips · at each stretto the singers' outlines light in turn.
2. **The Imp** (kept) · as drawn (D383).
3. **The Stone-Skipper** · Little Red Riding Hood skipping a stone across a pond, basket on her arm · pond-green and hood-red · rings spread at each entry; the proud close, one last skip.
4. **The Cross** · a great wooden cross on a hill at dusk, five candles at its foot · dusk-violet and candle · each new subject lights more candles; the last chord, all five.
5. **The Coronation** · a crown lowered onto a bowed head in a cathedral, banners above (a queen or king: the owner's gender rule) · purple and gilt · the dotted rhythms swing the banners; the last chord, the crown set.
6. **The Ambush** · a rider on a forest road, figures half-hidden behind the trees · forest-black and steel · each entry, a figure appears between the trees.
7. **The Bicycle** · a girl on an old high bicycle, ribbon flying, ringing the bell · lane-green and sky · the capricious break, the bell.
8. **The Pilgrim** · a pilgrim with staff and shell on a long road at dusk, a shrine far off · dust and dusk · each entry, a waymark passed; the end, the shrine lit.
9. **The Sparrow** (kept) · as drawn.
10. **The Horsemen** (kept) · as drawn.
11. **The Shepherdess** (kept) · as drawn; the owner asked earlier to fix her face.
12. **The Requiem** · a choir in black behind a bier, candles in a row · black and candle · each entry lights a candle; the last chord, all.
13. **The Water Mill** (round two D) · a mill wheel turning slowly beside a brook · brook-green and wood · the wheel turns once a bar. The owner noted "The Aquarium": to confirm whether it replaces The Water Mill.
14. **Pierrot** (kept) · as drawn.
15. **The Pinball** · a pinball machine, the ball mid-flight among lit bumpers · neon and chrome · each entry lights a bumper; the final cadence, TILT dims everything.
16. **The Knight** (kept) · as drawn.
17. **The Diary** · a girl writing in a diary by a summer window, a pressed flower on the page · summer-gold and faded-blue · at the end the diary closes.
18. **The Sleepwalker** (kept) · as drawn; the owner asked earlier to improve the face and hat.
19. **The Three Stooges** (the owner's decision, after the studio's caution about the trademark and likenesses) · three slapstick comedians in the look of a silent film · sepia and flicker strips · a small screen; the end, "FIN". The studio draws three generic pixel figures, not portraits.
20. **The Locomotive** · a steam locomotive at full power, pistons driving, smoke streaming · coal-black and boiler-red · driving wheels turn with the bar; inverted entries turn the other wheel; over the closing pedal, the whistle and the brakes.
21. **The Marionettes** · three marionettes dancing in step, strings rising out of frame · puppet-stage red and gold · each entry, one dances; the cadences, all bow.
22. **The Procession** (kept) · as drawn.
23. **The Gramophone** · an old gramophone with a horn in a quiet room, a cat asleep · wood and brass-horn · at the end the needle lifts.
24. **Faust** · a scholar in her or his study at night, a book open, a sign glowing in the air · study-brown and sigil · the sign brightens as the voices climb; at the end, it fades.

## Book II

1. **The Olympian** · an athlete on a podium, laurel crowned, arms up: a woman or androgynous (the owner) · marble and laurel · the close's high spirits, the laurel shines.
2. **The Shadows** · figures walking at sunset, their shadows twice their length (the subject at double length, D383) · sunset and long-shadow · the augmented entry, a shadow stretches.
3. **The Switchboard** · an operator at an old telephone switchboard, cords crossing · brass and cord · each entry plugs a cord; the inverted answer plugs upside down.
4. **The Perpetual Motion Machine** · a wooden wheel with swinging weights, its inventor proudly beside it · wood and brass · it turns all through the piece and never stops.
5. **The Bell Tower** · a bell tower at evening, one bell swinging, others waiting · stone and bronze · each entry swings a bell; the final stretto (bars 43–45), all four.
6. **The Catapult** · a siege catapult at the instant of release · timber and stone · each entry, the arm draws back; the run, released.
7. **The Organist** (kept) · as drawn.
8. **The Rain** · rain falling on a dark street, one lit window · wet-slate · the repeated opening notes as drops on the sill.
9. **The Chorale** · a congregation standing, hymn books open, singing · pew-wood and window-light · each entry, a line of the hymn lit.
10. **The Crook** (the owner's name) · a pickpocket in a long coat lifting a purse at a crowded fair · night-alley and lantern · each entry, a lantern goes out.
11. **The Butterfly** (kept) · as drawn.
12. **The Masquerade** · a masked ball in the vein of the secret ball in Kubrick's Eyes Wide Shut: masks, cloaks, a candlelit hall · ballroom-black and mask-gold · the fan opens at each cadence.
13. **The Telephone** · a rotary telephone on a table, its bell shaking · telephone-black and brass · the opening trill, the bell shakes.
14. **The Three Sisters** (the owner's name) · three sisters of three ages under one tree · three colours on the strips · each subject adds its sister; combined, all three (three subjects combined, the note).
15. **Hopscotch** (the owner's name) · a girl hopping across chalk squares · pavement and chalk · each bar a square (Bodky: counted in whole bars; Riemann's zig-zag).
16. **The Jury** (the owner's name) · a jury box, the forewoman on her feet, the others shouting · courtroom-wood · the subject's four repeated notes, the verdict hammered; the end, silence.
17. **The Mirror** · a woman before a tall mirror, her reflection looking back, symmetrical · silver and candle · the mirror's frame across both strips; the two halves light alike.
18. **The Gigue** · a couple dancing a jig on a tavern floor, a fiddler on a barrel · tavern-wood and candle · the bow once a bar of 6/8; both themes together, the couple spins.
19. **The Wanderer** (kept) · as drawn.
20. **The Earthquake** · a city street splitting, a lamp-post leaning · rubble and dust · each entry, the crack lengthens.
21. **The Cradle** · a mother (or father) rocking a cradle by a fire · fire-warm and wood · rocks once a bar; the close, still.
22. **The Colossus** · a giant statue over a harbour, ships small at its feet · harbour-bronze and sea · each entry, the statue firmer.
23. **The Elegy** · a poet reading by a grave under a willow · willow-green and stone · branches move slowly; the end, still.
24. **The Last Dance** · two dancers in an emptying ballroom, candles burning low · ballroom and candle · the candles lower over the piece; the end, one candle.
